The App

Everything you need to grow. Nothing to distract you.

FaithShift chooses one word from Scripture for you each day, then gives you simple, gentle ways to live it out.

Weekly challenges

Seven days of small, faithful actions, a new challenge each week, and a celebration when you finish.

A word for how you feel

Anxious, worn out, heartbroken or starting over? Receive Scripture and encouragement for exactly that.

The whole Bible

World English Bible and King James Version, searchable and readable offline. Save verses and journal about them.

Growth goals

Set a goal in any area of life, anchor it to Scripture, and take it one step at a time.

A private journal

Reflections, prayers, gratitude and wins, kept on your iPhone.

A Scripture-grounded coach

Bring the questions on your heart to a warm AI coach that answers with Scripture and practical next steps.
