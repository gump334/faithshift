How it works

One word, chosen for you, every day.

No endless scrolling and no shuffle button. FaithShift picks today’s verse and principle, and guides you through four small steps.

  1. Receive

    A verse from Scripture, a short principle in plain words, and why it matters today.

  2. Reflect

    One honest question to sit with. Write a few lines, or reflect quietly.

  3. Live it out

    Three small, practical steps. Doing just one completes the day.

  4. Encourage

    Pass today’s word on to someone who needs it, with a message ready to send.

Four areas of life

Faith for every part of your week.

The daily word rotates through all four areas, so nothing gets left behind. A full year of principles, and no verse repeats.

Career

Work as worship, skill as service, and wisdom for the hard days.

Life

Faith, peace of mind, purpose, rest and the habits that grow you.

Relationships

Love that listens, forgives and builds others up.

Finances

Faithful stewardship, contentment, generosity and wise planning.
