# FaithShift

**Small Steps, Real Change.**

FaithShift is an iPhone app that helps you slow down and apply Bible
principles to four areas of life — career, life, relationships, and
finances — so you grow and feel encouraged every day.

Every morning the app chooses a word from Scripture for you: a verse, a
principle, and three small steps. Reflect on it, live it out, and pass it on.
