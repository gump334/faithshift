Using FaithShift

Why does everyone get the same word each day?

FaithShift chooses one verse and principle for each day, shared by everyone, and a new one arrives at midnight in your time zone. There’s no shuffle button on purpose: the app chooses, and you receive it. The daily word rotates through career, life, relationships and finances, and no verse repeats for a full year.

Which Bible translations are included?

The World English Bible (WEB) and the King James Version (KJV), both built into the app and readable offline. Each chapter also has a “Read in NLT” link that opens the New Living Translation on Bible Gateway.

My progress disappeared. What happened?

Your progress is saved on your iPhone. If you deleted and reinstalled the app, or moved to a new phone without iCloud sync, it may not carry over. Make sure you’re signed in to iCloud on your iPhone. If something still looks wrong, email us and we’ll help.

Subscriptions

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose FaithShift. You can also open Progress → Manage Subscription in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid the next charge.

I paid but don’t see my plan. How do I restore it?

Open Progress in FaithShift and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

When do my coach messages reset?

Plus includes 30 coach messages a month and Premium includes 300. Your count resets at the start of each calendar month. The Coach tab always shows how many you have left.

The coach

Is the coach a real person?

No. The coach is an AI that answers with Scripture and practical steps. It can make mistakes, and it isn’t a substitute for your pastor, a counselor, a doctor or a financial professional.

Does the coach remember my conversations?

Your conversations are kept on your iPhone. Each time you send a message, recent conversation is sent along so the coach can answer in context. Our server stores only message counts, not what you wrote.

The coach says it’s resting.

Occasionally the coach is briefly unavailable. Everything else in FaithShift keeps working, and your message isn’t counted. Please try again a little later.

Your data

Where is my journal stored?

On your iPhone, and in your private iCloud if sync is on. We don’t have a server that stores your journal, prayers or progress.

How do I delete my data?

In FaithShift, open Progress and tap Erase all my data, then tap again to confirm. This permanently removes everything the app stored. See our Privacy Policy for details.
